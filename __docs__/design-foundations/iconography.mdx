# Iconography

HSK AI uses a single, coherent icon language across learner PWA and Android. The standard UI icon family is **Phosphor Icons**; established HSK AI-specific symbols may remain as deliberately maintained custom assets. Iconography supports lesson clarity, navigation and feedback. It does not replace canonical Panda/character artwork, correct/incorrect answer reaction effects or the AI Voice Orb; those are governed by the Illustration and Motion rules.

## Icon library

**Phosphor Icons** is the HSK AI canonical icon family for standard actions, navigation, settings, forms and status indicators. Use **Regular**, **Bold** and **Fill** weights only; avoid Thin, Light and decorative Duotone in normal product UI. Keep icon geometry and meaning consistent between PWA and Android; each platform may render the approved icon with its native technology without adding a second visual style.

New standard icons must follow this rule immediately. Existing production icons are checked against this rule during the full PWA/Android UI audit and aligned during the controlled component migration. This documentation change does not itself require an untested bulk replacement in live product code.

Do not change canonical HSK AI Panda illustrations or other approved characters into generic Phosphor icons.

## Sizes and weights

| Visual icon size | Weight | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 px | Bold | Compact decorative-adjacent indicators where legible |
| 16 px | Bold | Input fields, inline status, badges |
| 20 px | Bold | Buttons, compact messages, back/close controls when appropriately framed |
| 24 px | Regular | Icon buttons, navigation, learning utilities |
| 48 px | Regular | Large standalone symbolic illustration or empty-state icon |

Use the nearest approved size rather than creating an arbitrary per-screen size. A visually small icon can live inside a much larger tap target.

## Usage guidelines

### Use Phosphor for generic UI icons

All new ordinary UI icons come from Phosphor, unless the symbol is a verified, established HSK AI-specific asset without a suitable Phosphor equivalent. Do not add a second generic icon library for one feature. A platform-native back gesture or operating-system control may retain its expected behavior without changing the shared information hierarchy.

### Labels and accessibility

Icons with ambiguous meanings must be paired with visible text or a clearly available explanation. Every meaningful icon needs an accessible name (for example aria-label on PWA and contentDescription on Android); decorative icons must not be announced by assistive technology.

Meaningful icon artwork must meet at least 3:1 contrast against its background. Keep clear keyboard/focus and selected states; do not use color or opacity alone to express an interactive state.

### States and visual weight

Use **Regular** and **Bold** for resting UI. Reserve **Fill** for genuinely selected/active states, not emphasis for its own sake. Do not wrap simple utility icons in decorative tiles, extra shadow layers or repeated icon backplates unless a functional selected/primary state requires a surface.

### Touch targets on HSK AI mobile platforms

Interactive touch areas must be at least **44 × 44 CSS px** on iPhone PWA and **48 × 48 dp** on Android, even when the icon drawing itself is 16, 20 or 24 px/dp. Respect safe areas, nearby controls and keyboard insets.

### Correct and incorrect lesson feedback

Use a clear success indicator (e.g. a check) and a different clear incorrect/error indicator (e.g. an X) with text or another accessible cue. The existing Panda/other approved-character facial reactions, gestures, animations and feedback effects after **both correct and incorrect answers are retained**. Icons are an additional accessibility cue, not a reason to remove character feedback. Incorrect-answer feedback must never shame the learner.

## HSK AI-specific custom icons

Product-specific symbols such as approved HSK levels, mastery/progress, XP/streak, exercise types or already-established reward assets may remain custom **only when an existing canonical source asset is verified**. Reuse that same symbol and name across platforms. Do not copy Khan Academy's Gems, class, teacher, parent or Khanmigo icons; these are another brand's product vocabulary.

If no approved HSK AI-specific custom asset exists, use the closest semantically correct Phosphor icon with a readable label rather than generating a new decorative glyph.

Panda, approved characters, and the red pearlescent AI Voice Orb are **not** generic icons. They keep their original artwork, expressions, motion and appearance under the Illustration and Motion rules.

## Roles and responsibilities

Icon selection is an interaction design decision; a custom icon is a brand design decision. New custom assets require a clear product need and must fit the established HSK AI visual system. Cross-platform rendering is verified during the PWA/Android UI audit, including sizes, contrast, labels, hit targets and correct/incorrect lesson feedback.
