# Recovered professional visual references

> **SOURCE REFERENCE — NOT HSK AI UI OR PRODUCT RULES.** These professional diagrams were safely recovered from this repository's historical revision `f5ce49c`. They were created for a different educational product and may include its colors, wording, brand marks and school workflows. They must never be copied into the HSK AI app, treated as the approved HSK AI palette, or used to justify unsupported features. The diagrams are **non-authoritative instructional examples**, not screenshots of the current PWA or Android application.

The `main` branch's HSK AI rules, `AGENTS.md`, canonical character system and server-verified data contracts remain authoritative. For new work, prefer their related guideline pages, then use a figure here **only to understand a reusable professional design principle**. Do not revive upstream school, Gems, Khanmigo, Khanductor or teacher-specific rules.

## 1. Semantic colors and contrast

The first original diagrams explain *contrast and semantic color structure*, not which exact colors HSK AI must use.

## Recovery and review rules

1. The restored PNG files are **historical source examples**. The gallery shows a curated subset of the recovered images; other assets remain available in their original paths for controlled reference, not automatic use.
2. Do not reintroduce source-only character illustrations, Gem challenges, schools, teacher workflows, score thresholds, source color tokens or original proprietary icon demands.
3. The four React demonstrations are **documentation-only fictional data** and require no HSK AI application services. They do not define backend events or release a new analytics screen.
4. Before copying a principle into HSK AI, check the actual PWA/Android code, account authorization, payment status, exercise answer reveal behavior, typography, themes and accessibility.
5. In any implementation, verify CSS/Android responsive behavior, keyboard interaction, missing versus zero, screen-reader summary and reduced motion. Do not call the demonstration a tested production app feature.
6. `AGENTS.md` and the adapted HSK AI guidelines have precedence over this gallery if wording conflicts.
