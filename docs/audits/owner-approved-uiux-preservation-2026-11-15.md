## Preservation rules for this restoration

1. **Never edit the above owner-approved original text**, original Library artifact, or implemented HSK AI production features as part of importing historic Design System references. All proposed additions and source-vs-product conflicts belong in *separate* audit notes in this working branch.
2. **Preserve professional designer reasoning** from the original source: exact when/when-not usage, hierarchy, spacing, interaction semantics, accessibility, state and motion guidance. A branded example may be rewritten as a **clearly labeled HSK AI design proposal**, not a claim of existing behavior; the general principle must survive.
3. **Order of evidence:** later explicitly owner-approved decisions in the original > verified current HSK AI user-facing behavior and project contracts > proposed design-system adaptations > third-party drawings or archived decisions. When the first two disagree, **log a conflict and do not silently edit either**. A technical discrepancy between an approval and current code is not permission to rewrite the approval.
4. Distinguish **approved / explicitly recorded / conditionally approved / unapproved / future implementation**. Never promote a proposal to approved on the strength of a third-party pattern or a Storybook mock.
5. Prefer current **real code** when describing existing screens: `HSK-AI-Bot/main` PWA `course-v3.html`, `course_v3_data/design-tokens.css`, real iPhone onboarding & Voice V8, Android `Color.kt`, `LessonCharacters.kt`, `VoiceV8Orb.kt`, plus `AGENTS.md`, `AI_RULES.md` and `.agents/skills/hsk-ai-ui/SKILL.md`. Record code SHA and platform differences where material; product behavior is never inferred from a Design System MDX.
6. Do not treat historic source's school roles, mascot exclusivity, Wonder Blocks tokens, Gems thresholds, official verdict owners or governance machinery as active HSK AI instructions. Useful general professional lessons can be preserved with source/provenance and reworded *only in new adaptation notes*.
7. Before any main promotion: item-level review against the original history; no changed owner-approval text; working Storybook build, MDX/links/assets, keyboard/focus/mobile visuals, accessibility; licensing/provenance of third-party image assets. If CI or visual/IP checks are unavailable, report them as unverified.
8. No changes to HSK-AI-Bot `main`, payment, entitlement, auth, lesson runtime, analytics, notifications or monitoring for this guideline recovery.
