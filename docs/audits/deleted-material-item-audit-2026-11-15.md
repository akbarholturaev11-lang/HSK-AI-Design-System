**Conflicts discovered and disposition:**

1. Source `Surfaces and elevations` required `semanticColor.core.background.*` and a four-level `boxShadow.*` design system. **Contradicts actual code.** Active historical page now maps conceptual layers to actual PWA tokens, preserves external diagrams only as examples and avoids adding non-existent shadow variables.
2. Source `Khanmigo` exclusive assistant and `Khanductor` proctor policies **contradict existing multi-character and lesson-answer behavior**. Retain transferable rules about non-obstructive guidance and independent answers; keep source personas inactive.
3. School assignment/gems/scoring visuals **contradict product measurements**. The restored interactive HSK mock demos are explicitly fictional and must not drive analytics/XP.
4. Original global elevation/shadow recommendations conflict with **flat-by-default** utility/navigation and icon rules. Do not add backplates or nested cards merely to mimic reference images.
5. Original desktop/modal concepts cannot replace PWA safe-area navigation, keyboard-aware sheets, or Android native back/IME behavior.
6. Source governance verdicts naming a third-party Design Infra team cannot become HSK AI ownership or approval policy. General lessons about API-supported layout overrides, a surface not being a Card, and accessible icon-only controls can be adapted without importing the committee's enforcement workflow.

**Preserve fixed product decisions:** Panda/cast visuals, correct and wrong reactions, immersive Voice Orb, bottom navigation, two-line optional disclosure with top-right chevron, Safari onboarding, verified account/payment states, analytics/monitoring constraints, and active PWA + Android product scope. No code edits were made to `HSK-AI-Bot`.
